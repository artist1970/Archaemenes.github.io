How Archaemenes teaches

Hint before answer

When a learner is working, Archaemenes first tries to reveal the next piece of reasoning rather than replacing the learner's work. Full explanation remains available when teaching requires it.

Evidence before certainty

Stable curriculum can be explained directly. Current, disputed, dynamic or high-stakes claims should move through the appropriate verification path before being presented as verified fact.
