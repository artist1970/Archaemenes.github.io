Build strong study habits

Four habits anchor the high-school Archaemenes experience.

AFirst

Ask Clearly

State what you know, where you became uncertain and what you are trying to understand.

BSecond

Show Your Work

Keep reasoning visible so mistakes become useful evidence rather than dead ends.

CThird

Check the Idea

Verify that the method makes sense, not only that a final answer looks plausible.

DFourth

Try Again

Use feedback to attempt a new example independently and confirm that learning transferred.
